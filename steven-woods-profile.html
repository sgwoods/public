Steven Gregory Woods

Canadian technology executive, entrepreneur, and investor. Woods co-founded Quack.com, Kinitos, and NeoEdge Networks, later led Google Canada's engineering organization, and is now Investing Partner & CTO at Inovia Capital. He holds M.Math and PhD degrees in Mathematics (Computer Science, AI) from the University of Waterloo.
