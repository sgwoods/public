Partner & CTO Inovia Capital. X-Google. From Saskatchewan. Canadian. Dr

Short Bio

I am a team builder, engineering leader, founder and X-Googler. Currently I am Partner and CTO at Inovia Capital (https://www.inovia.vc/team/steve-woods/) . A long time ago I co-founded a few companies including Quack.com (https://quack.com/)  which we sold to a very large internet company of ill-repute.

I hail from, and spend a lot of time up in northern Saskatchewan. I enjoy the wilderness of the north, and take the odd photograph while up there.

I currently reside in the countryside with my family near Waterloo, Ontario, Canada.

I have a very active interest in recent developments in artificial intelligence and in a different life finished a PhD on the topic at the University of Waterloo (https://uwaterloo.ca/math/people-profiles/steven-woods) .
