# Steven Gregory Woods timeline

Updated: April 1, 2026

This is the current working person-level timeline for the Steven Woods archive. It is intentionally conservative and uses approximate dates where the source base is still being tightened.

## Education and early research

- `1987`:
  - B.Sc. in Computer Science, University of Saskatchewan.
- `1991-1992`:
  - Canberra period in Australia, combining CSIRO work and Canberra Knights hockey.
- `1993-1996`:
  - Graduate work in Mathematics (Computer Science) at the University of Waterloo.
- `1996`:
  - PhD awarded at the University of Waterloo.
- `1996-1997`:
  - Postdoctoral studies at the University of Hawai'i at Manoa.

## Research and software engineering phase

- `1998-1999`:
  - Software Engineering Institute period in Pittsburgh.
  - Architecture and software reengineering work remains an active archive lane.

## Startup and company-building phase

- `1999-2000`:
  - Quack.com co-founder period.
  - Public record clearly captures the AOL acquisition in 2000.
- `2000-2008`:
  - Kinitos and later NeoEdge Networks.
  - This stretch includes software services, infrastructure, gaming, and advertising technology.

## Google Canada phase

- `2008-2021`:
  - Google Canada engineering and product leadership.
  - Public first-party and university sources describe this as the phase in which the Google Canada technical team grew from about 20 to more than 1,300.

## Inovia phase

- `2021-08 to present`:
  - Partner and CTO at Inovia Capital.
  - Current public role combines company-building, founder support, technical evaluation, and investment context.

## Recognition anchors

- `1996`:
  - IEEE Working Conference on Reverse Engineering Outstanding Contribution Award.
- `2010`:
  - University of Waterloo J.W. Graham Medal.
- `2021`:
  - University of Saskatchewan Lifetime Achievement Award.
