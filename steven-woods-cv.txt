Steven Gregory Woods
Investing Partner & CTO, Inovia Capital  |  Waterloo, Ontario, Canada

Canadian technology executive, founder, inventor, and investor. Investing Partner & CTO at Inovia
Capital, former leader of Google Canada's engineering organization, and co-founder of Quack.com,
Kinitos, and NeoEdge Networks. PhD and M.Math in Mathematics (Computer Science, AI), University of
Waterloo.

LEADERSHIP EXPERIENCE
- 2021-present  Investing Partner & CTO, Inovia Capital: Leads investing, technical diligence, AI
  strategy, and portfolio company support.
- 2008-2021  Google Canada engineering leadership: Led Google Canada's engineering organization from
  about 20 to more than 1,300 technical staff.
- 2000-2008  Co-founder and executive, Kinitos / NeoEdge Networks: Built and deployed products across
  enterprise software, internet services, gaming, and advertising technology.
- 1999-2000  Co-founder, Quack.com: Built an early voice systems startup acquired by AOL in 2000.
- 1998-1999  Software Engineering Institute, Carnegie Mellon University: Advanced software architecture,
  design recovery, and program understanding.
- 1991-1992  CSIRO, Canberra, Australia: Research and development for CSIRO Australia; Canberra Knights
  semi-professional player.
