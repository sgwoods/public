Release notes

v0.1.14-beta · 2026-10-23

  • First-lines check for the daily briefing: the day in brief and Top of mind are now checked against the edition's own body, as deep-dive summaries have been since v0.1.9 (consistency.check_briefing). A figure belongs to its whole story, so a line can name the company in its lead. A sentence that ties a figure to the wrong company, or says something wasn't disclosed when the body gives it, is dropped.
  • Across days (flag): a "not disclosed" claim is compared with the previous 14 editions and flagged for review if an earlier day gave the figure for the same company.
  • The "not disclosed" test is stricter everywhere: a question asking for a figure, or a sentence saying it's lacking, no longer counts as giving it, and it must be about the same company.
  • ./np consistency [DAY|all] (also a run-queue recipe) runs the checks over published editions as a dry run. Over all 18 editions and 8 deep dives it found one misattributed figure (10-22's day in brief) and two cross-day flags (10-23).

v0.1.13-beta · 2026-10-23

  • Fix: the run queue's launchd tick never started a job. Its quick idle check listed both queue folders with ls, which fails whenever one of them is empty. It now checks each file, and a test runs the real ./np runner tick.

How it is made

Curated sources

Lab and company posts, trade press, researchers and newsletters, every feed verified live before it is used.

Evidence first

Each story is weighed by what kind of source backs it; every Must read gets an evidence check, and figures that trace to no source are looked up or cut before anything is sent.

Local first

Runs each morning with local models for most stages and Claude for the critical pass and editing; voiced locally.

Release gate

Nothing goes out (site, podcast, email) unless tests, a secrets scan, privacy checks and a human approval of sensitive changes have passed. Changes reach production as releases.

Current focus

Beta: aggressive fact checking before email and audio, wider source coverage; podcast listing after four green weeks.
