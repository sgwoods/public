v0.2.24-beta · 2026-10-28

  • Phone notifications and one-tap approvals (Steven, 10-28: "more control from my iPhone"): through the free, open-source ntfy app. Approvals arrive as a notification with Approve and Reject buttons; one tap, no passphrase, no browser. The tap sends a one-time token the Mac checks, over private topics only the Mac's Keychain and the phone know. Nudges: the takes page is ready (tap opens it), the evening deep-dive note is out (tap opens the queue page), a run needs attention, a run-queue job finished. Turn on with the ntfy-setup job (a Mac Approve; the topic lands on the clipboard for the phone), then "ntfy test". The passphrase path stays as a fallback. Security-sensitive: a third approval path.

How it is made

Curated sources

Lab and company posts, trade press, researchers and newsletters, every feed verified live before it is used.

Evidence first

Each story is weighed by what kind of source backs it; every Must read gets an evidence check, and figures that trace to no source are looked up or cut before anything is sent.

Local first

Runs each morning with local models for most stages and Claude for the critical pass and editing; voiced locally.

Release gate

Nothing goes out (site, podcast, email) unless tests, a secrets scan, privacy checks and a human approval of sensitive changes have passed. Changes reach production as releases.

Current focus

Beta: aggressive fact checking before email and audio, wider source coverage; podcast listing after four green weeks.
