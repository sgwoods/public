  • "Who they are" (Steven, 2026-11-04; pipeline/intros.py): the first time the briefing names a company, one or two sentences on what it does and how it differs from or complements the well-known players; a company back after two weeks (analysis.who_they_are_days) gets a one-sentence reminder that builds on our earlier lines (kept in data/state/companies.json) and changes only where the company has.   • Fact checks in the takes email (pipeline/factopts.py): the pre-send check, the deep-dive check and the accuracy audit (now run before the takes email, not after sending) are listed at the bottom as F1, F2 ... each with A/B/C options and a recommendation. Steven answers in the same reply ("F1: B"); unanswered items get the recommendation. Choices are applied to the edition and the deep dive before publishing, and the podcast is re-recorded if it changed.
  • The home page says plainly whether anything is waiting on Steven (11-04): a green "Nothing is waiting on you" or a red "Waiting on you: N decisions" heads the page; a release or other job in progress shows as "Running now" with its step in words ("step 3 of 8, rebuilding today's edition with the new code") instead of "Queued, waiting"; queued jobs and items worth a look say "Nothing for you to do". Today's deep-dive fact-check items stop showing once the edition is sent (they are choices in the takes email now).
  • Campaigns (pipeline/campaigns.py, dashboard Audience → Campaigns): invite lists with each person's status worked out from the subscriber list (invited, signed up, subscribed, abandoned, declined) and their replies, the funnel and conversion. The friends trial (Nov 2026) is set up with its invite; people are added from the dashboard.
  • Special editions (Steven, 2026-11-04; pipeline/special.py, ./np special): an occasional extra deep dive that goes out on its own the day news breaks, dated that day; the daily deep dive is untouched. Draft (written and checked, not voiced) → a review email to Steven with the article and its fact checks as F1, F2 ... (A/B/C, a recommendation) → his notes or choices → Release (chat, or the button on the dashboard home page): recorded, its page at <site>/DAY/special/ (in no podcast feed), then one email to every active subscriber once the page is live (07:00-21:00). It opens "This is an AI Briefing special edition for ..." with no hand-off. At most one a day and four in 30 days. Run-queue recipes: special-draft, special-revise, special-decide, special-release.
  • Fewer good sources dropped as dead (11-04 trial over 24 dropped links): a link check retries once (10 of 24 were fine on a retry) and GETs where a server refuses HEAD; a page the plain fetch can't read gets one try in the Mac's Chrome, headless (pipeline/headless.py; 5 of 24: bot checks, Bluesky posts that need JavaScript), only where robots.txt allows and only public hosts, with a throwaway profile and a cap per run. Pages behind real walls (paywalls, Cloudflare; 9 of 24) stay out. Off with fetch.headless: false.

How it is made

Curated sources

Lab and company posts, trade press, researchers and newsletters, every feed verified live before it is used.

Evidence first

Each story is weighed by what kind of source backs it; every Must read gets an evidence check, and figures that trace to no source are looked up or cut before anything is sent.

Local first

Runs each morning with local models for most stages and Claude for the critical pass and editing; voiced locally.

Release gate

Nothing goes out (site, podcast, email) unless tests, a secrets scan, privacy checks and a human approval of sensitive changes have passed. Changes reach production as releases.

Current focus

Beta: aggressive fact checking before email and audio, wider source coverage; podcast listing after four green weeks.
