How it is made

Curated sources

Lab and company posts, trade press, researchers and newsletters, every feed verified live before it is used.

Evidence first

Each story is weighed by what kind of source backs it; every Must read gets an evidence check, and figures that trace to no source are looked up or cut before anything is sent.

Local first

Runs each morning with local models for most stages and Claude for the critical pass and editing; voiced locally.

Release gate

Nothing goes out (site, podcast, email) unless tests, a secrets scan, privacy checks and a human approval of sensitive changes have passed. Changes reach production as releases.

Current focus

Beta: aggressive fact checking before email and audio, wider source coverage; podcast listing after four green weeks.
