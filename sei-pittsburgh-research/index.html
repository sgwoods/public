Repository Workflow

01 Incoming intake

New SEI finds land in `incoming/` first so raw captures are logged before they are normalized into archive state.

02 Era source review

Stable SEI-era sources are promoted into `source-manifest.json` with dates, URLs, statuses, and notes that explain why they matter to the era archive.

03 Local preservation

Preserved local copies live in `historic/artifacts/archive-html/` and `historic/artifacts/archive-pdf/` so the era archive does not depend only on live URLs or the Steven layer.
